# Game-First Product Re-Baseline

**Decision:** Approved by the product owner on 2026-08-24.

This decision supersedes earlier product direction wherever that direction makes comic capture, production, publication, reading, or editing part of the player product. World canon, contract authority, viewpoint safety, Ledger Time, autonomous actors, persistent consequences, accessibility, and the authoritative action workflow remain binding.

## Experience promise

Mythic is a cinematic tabletop superhero adventure: enter a dangerous living scene, understand what matters, attempt anything plausible, and watch a persistent world answer in memorable ways.

## Locked direction

- The Adaptive Living World Stage is scene-first, not an equal-weight dashboard.
- The reference interaction is cinematic tabletop play.
- Primary navigation is **Play**, **Hero**, and **World**.
- Allies, Gear, Journal, settings, recovery, and audit are contextual or secondary destinations.
- Comic generation, capture status, queue, issue library, reader, editor, production providers, publication, and comic onboarding are removed from product scope.
- **Journal** preserves player-knowable choices and consequences. Optional History/Rules Audit exposes deeper authoritative detail.
- Free intent remains always available, supplemented by no more than three contextual examples that never define legal action space.
- Goal and method remain authoritative internal concepts but ordinary play uses one natural action expression.
- Clear low-risk actions avoid redundant confirmation. Material ambiguity, irreversible cost, and meaningful danger receive a correctable interpretation and deliberate commit.
- Concise outcomes are the default. Guided explanation and rules audit are disclosed on request.
- QA fixtures, projection status, state versions, receipt identifiers, and knowledge-taxonomy labels are not ordinary player-facing chrome.

## Vertical-slice boundary

The next slice proves one polished 10–15 minute Drowned Archive sequence: arrival, perception, Ilya interaction, reflection investigation, unusual action, proportionate interpretation, authoritative resolution, visible consequence, and follow-up. Breadth follows demonstrated fun.

## Acceptance signals

A first-time player can identify place, people, pressure, goal, and available agency within 30 seconds and makes a consequential choice within 90 seconds. The scene receives dominant visual attention; rules and audit never compete with play. Usability participants should primarily describe the experience as a superhero RPG, living-world adventure, or cinematic tabletop game—not a dashboard, CRM, admin tool, database, or chatbot.
