## 2. Visual-Asset Contract

Representational assets use the approved Harbor Civic Myth hierarchy: ambient world, scene, entity, object, event and comic. Industrial Reliquary enriches Relics, custody, repair and material consequence; Sequential Atlas appears only for authored transitions, Chronicle and comic production. Decorative map texture may sit beneath semantic map geometry but can never own routes, topology, labels, discovery or uncertainty.

Every meaningful asset slot names its screen/scenario contexts, canonical scope, knowledge ceiling, generation method, formats, crop rule, alt contract, structured fallback, rights state, review owner and immutable replacement rule. Generated or supplied art never contains live interface labels, dialogue, receipts, maps, exact values or other changeable text.

## 4. Code-Native Icon and Spatial Rule

The package includes 49 text-free SVG symbols covering six destinations; knowledge classes; ownership/custody/access; pressure; harm; comic-production states; and public UI states. Symbols use a 24×24 canvas, 1.75px stroke and `currentColor`. Every first use retains visible text. Icon-only controls require a localized accessible name. Filled variants are restricted to selected, destructive or immediately due state.

Focus, state cues, maps, charts, routes, evidence geometry, comic status and fallbacks remain semantic HTML/CSS/SVG. Art may disappear under forced colors, reduced data, failure or user preference without removing information or capability.

## 5. Screen Placement and Progressive Disclosure

All 14 UI-02 screen families have an explicit asset policy and a named fallback. Campaign Entry and Issue #0 use optional atmosphere to introduce premise. The Stage uses place/entity/object art while speech, geography, actions and pressure remain structured. Forecast never pre-renders an uncommitted outcome. Character and Company surfaces require exact entity versions. World raster layers remain decorative beneath knowledge-safe map geometry. Chronicle art requires Inflection, Source Lock, a Canon Snapshot, Reader Knowledge Ceiling, rights/consent review and immutable edition lineage. Settings and recovery remain fully functional without representational art.

## 6. Delivery and Failure Behavior

Lossless sRGB masters produce immutable AVIF/WebP derivatives with PNG fallback. Stage/art-direction widths cover 640, 960, 1280, 1672 and 2400; cutouts cover 256, 512 and 1024. Only the chosen LCP plate may load eagerly. Every other image reserves dimensions, loads progressively and resolves to a semantic fallback without blocking legal intent. Compact, 200% zoom, reduced-data, forced-colors and screen-reader modes preserve the same capability.

Public clients receive only delivery media, public alt text and a public lineage token. Prompts, private reference paths, protected audit data, hidden rejection notes, consent records and rights evidence stay server-side.
