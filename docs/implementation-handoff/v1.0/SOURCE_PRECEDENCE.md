# Source Precedence and Conflict Rules

1. **World Constitution** governs canon, world invariants and player-visible truth.
2. **Approved controlled decisions and UX/UI Foundation** govern product experience, Ledger Time, Adaptive Living World Stage, progressive disclosure and desktop-first behavior.
3. **C1–C3 approved contracts** govern payload shapes, authority, versioning, knowledge, commands, receipts and events.
4. **C4 approved planning** governs traceability, prerequisites, CI gates and sequencing.
5. **UI-01 through UI-04 approved packages** govern components, screens/states, assets and frontend architecture.
6. **This handoff runbook** governs execution order and stop conditions but may not override the above.

Where authority is orthogonal, apply all requirements. Where two artifacts at the same level conflict, stop and report the exact contradiction instead of choosing silently. Historical planning handoffs are context only when a later approved artifact is more specific.
