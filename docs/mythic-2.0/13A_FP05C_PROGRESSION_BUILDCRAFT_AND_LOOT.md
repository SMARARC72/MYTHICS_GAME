# Mythic 2.0 — FP-05C Progression, Buildcraft and Loot

**Status:** LOCKED structural design; numeric balance remains tunable

## Progression promise

The game must create a satisfying RPG loop independent of AI novelty:

`challenge -> tactical/creative play -> consequence -> XP/loot/knowledge -> meaningful build decision -> new capability -> larger solution space`

Players should regularly have a near-term reward, meaningful build choices, visible improvement, equipment worth inspecting and long-term mythic aspirations.

## Progression dimensions

Mythic progression is multidimensional:

1. Level 1–20;
2. ability scores;
3. skill proficiency/expertise;
4. perks/talents;
5. weapon/equipment mastery;
6. powers/techniques;
7. equipment/resources;
8. relationships/reputation;
9. Power Tier;
10. Mantles/Names/Epithets;
11. property/business/institutional power;
12. Legacy/successor advantages produced by the world.

No single track replaces the others.

## Level and tier spine

Retain the immediately legible 1–20 level spine. Proficiency and Power Tier milestones align at 5/9/13/17:

- 1–4 Emergent
- 5–8 Established
- 9–12 Iconic
- 13–16 Mythic
- 17–20 Ascendant

Milestones should unlock materially new tactical/world possibilities rather than merely increase numbers.

## XP philosophy

XP comes from meaningful accomplishment, not only kills: opposition neutralized, exploration/discovery, objectives, investigation, social/political outcomes, survival, projects, invention/crafting, faction/world accomplishments and appropriate conviction/morality systems.

Killing can have value without making indiscriminate murder universally optimal. Repetition receives diminishing reward; genuinely evolved/new challenges can reset challenge value.

The older custom XP curve is a **candidate tuning baseline**, not automatically shipping math. Before coding exact coefficients, simulate hours-per-level, encounter rewards, major discovery/objective rewards, grinding, successor catch-up and high-level campaign pace.

## Build Points

Preserve flexible Build Points because Mythic supports characters beyond rigid fantasy classes. Candidate cadence from legacy work (4 BP/level plus milestone bonuses and periodic attribute improvements) is tuning data until build-cost simulation proves the choice density is healthy.

BP can buy proficiencies/expertise, perks, techniques, power enhancements, equipment mastery, reactions and utility/exploration capabilities. BP cannot buy arbitrary world facts.

## Origins/archetypes

Character creation uses Origin/background + readable starting Archetype + power source or mundane specialty + starting proficiencies/techniques/equipment/Drives where relevant.

Archetypes establish a clear starting identity (infiltrator, bruiser, marksman, investigator, mystic, technologist, leader, survivor, hybrids) but do not create hard class walls.

## Powers

Power families contain core permission/effect, potency/scale, control, reach/area, efficiency/resource cost, techniques, reactions, Stunt affordances and constraints/weaknesses.

Advancement should unlock new ways to use a power, not only more damage. Example: telekinesis can develop precision, forced movement, barriers, multi-target control, greater mass and environmental Stunts.

## Breakthroughs

Crossing 5/9/13/17 requires an in-world Breakthrough in addition to XP. Training, invention, exposure, pact/oath, transformation, research, crisis, theft/assimilation, spiritual realization or another coherent player-created approach can qualify. No predetermined heroic quest is required.

## Perks/masteries

Perks must change decisions: AP costs, reactions, weapon techniques, cover/terrain interaction, movement, Exertion rhythm, Stunt classes, morale/team play, etc. Avoid large trees of trivial percentage filler.

## Equipment

Equipment is persistent world property, not an abstract item-level treadmill. Progress comes from quality/material, condition, unique properties, technique access, build synergy, rarity/provenance, supernatural/technological traits, modification/crafting and faction/legal implications.

Sidegrades remain valuable: e.g., a lower-damage spear can be better for reach/brace/zone control.

## Loot

Enemies/locations provide what the world says is physically present or physiologically plausible. Do not generate unrelated random loot purely to feed dopamine.

Rewards can come from carried equipment, containers/caches, payment, salvage, harvested materials, information, claims/deeds, faction access, crafted rewards and relics/artifacts.

Excitement comes from rarity, provenance, tactical usefulness and world consequences.

## Unique items

Unique items have stable identity/history. They can be stolen, inherited, lost, broken, repaired, sold or recovered. The world never respawns an identical “unique” replacement for convenience.

## Crafting/projects

Crafting is systemic/project based: repair, modification, ammunition/consumables where relevant, invention, mythotechnology and artifact work. Large projects consume Ledger Time, facilities, skill, materials and collaborators and can be interrupted.

## Inventory friction

Track ownership/custody, carried/equipped/stored location, weight/bulk only to useful precision, containers, legal restrictions and condition/durability where meaningful. Avoid junk-stack spreadsheet punishment.

## Reward presentation

Important loot/levels/Tier changes get intentional visual/audio feedback and concise comparison showing the new tactical possibilities. Celebration should feel rewarding without becoming disruptive.

## Respec/retraining

No free instant respec that erases identity, but choices are not all permanently trapped. Retraining can occur through downtime, mentors/facilities, experimentation, powers/services and defined cost. Some origin/Mantle/oath/transformation choices can be irreversible unless the world contains a way to change them.

## Successors/Legacy

Successors do not automatically inherit Level/BP/powers. They may causally inherit wealth/property, equipment, mentors, reputation, faction access, blood/divine traits, institutions, taught knowledge, enemies and debts. An existing successor who lived/trained autonomously may already have appropriate experience and capabilities.

The world does not scale down for a weak heir; mitigation comes from safer areas, inherited resources, companions, training, social power, equipment or selecting a capable successor.

## Build diversity gates

Prototype at least several genuinely different builds and test whether they produce different movement, positioning, reaction, resource, equipment and Stunt decisions. If two builds mostly differ in damage coefficients, progression design fails.

## Lock

LOCKED: 1–20 progression spine; Power Tier milestones; multi-source XP; flexible build currency; meaningful perks/masteries; power technique families; physical persistent loot; unique item identity; project-based crafting; causal succession/Legacy; no item-level treadmill; no universal level scaling.

OPEN FOR TUNING: exact XP curve, BP costs/cadence, attribute-improvement cadence, perk catalog, rarity labels, inventory weight precision and final starting archetypes.
