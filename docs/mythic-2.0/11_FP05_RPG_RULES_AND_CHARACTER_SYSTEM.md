# Mythic 2.0 — FP-05 RPG Rules and Character System

**Status:** LOCKED baseline; numeric tuning remains open

## 1. Design target

Mythic uses familiar d20/5E grammar where familiarity reduces onboarding cost, while retaining Mythic-specific progression, scale, consequences, Fate, Mantles, Stunts and world simulation.

The rules system exists to make the game independently fun and legible. AI interprets unusual intent; it does not replace mechanics.

## 2. Core abilities

Mythic 2.0 uses six core abilities:

- Strength (STR)
- Dexterity (DEX)
- Constitution (CON)
- Intelligence (INT)
- Wisdom (WIS)
- Charisma (CHA)

Score modifier:

`modifier = floor((score - 10) / 2)`

This supersedes the older seven-attribute player model (Strength/Agility/Endurance/Intellect/Awareness/Willpower/Presence).

## 3. Universal resolution

Default check:

`d20 + ability modifier + proficiency/training + bounded situational modifiers vs DC/Defense`

Use Advantage/Disadvantage as the primary high-level circumstance mechanism. Edge/Setback is superseded for the Mythic 2.0 core.

Natural 20/1 can create critical/degree effects where the specific rule allows, but neither overrides impossibility or creates an unavailable capability.

## 4. Proficiency

Baseline Proficiency Bonus:

`PB = 2 + floor((Level - 1) / 4)`

- Levels 1–4: +2
- 5–8: +3
- 9–12: +4
- 13–16: +5
- 17–20: +6

Ordinary skill training:

- untrained: +0
- proficient: +PB
- expertise: +2PB

Skills may pair with different abilities when the player's method justifies it.

## 5. Skills

The final skill list remains a tuning/content decision, but it should cover athletics/physical force, acrobatics, stealth, perception, survival, investigation, medicine/science/technology where relevant, deception, persuasion, intimidation, insight and world-specific supernatural disciplines without creating dozens of redundant skills.

Skill choice does not constrain free intent; it is the mechanical interpretation of a method.

## 6. Saves and defense

Mythic uses six ability saving throws rather than the old universal Evasion/Fortitude/Resolve/Vigilance defense set.

Physical attacks usually resolve against **Defense/AC**. Armor, shields, cover, powers and conditions can alter that threshold or reduce effects according to explicit rules.

Mental, supernatural, environmental and bodily threats call for the appropriate ability save or explicit subsystem.

## 7. Hit points and injuries

HP represents short-term ability to remain in the fight, not literal uninjured flesh.

Large/severe hits, specific weapons/effects, reaching zero HP and other rules can create **persistent injuries/Wounds**. Wounds are authoritative world state and may outlast ordinary recovery.

No named actor receives undocumented plot armor.

## 8. Downed, dying and death

Reaching zero HP normally triggers a Downed/Dying evaluation for player characters and appropriate major actors rather than automatic deletion. Use a 5E-familiar stabilization/death-save structure or close equivalent for readability, with final numbers tuned in prototype.

Some explicitly lethal/destructive effects may bypass or modify the ordinary dying window when rules/fiction support it.

Death is canonical. Resurrection, afterlife, replacement bodies or mythic recovery follow explicit world rules and never rewind the event ledger.

## 9. Core resources

Mythic 2.0 streamlines universal player-facing resources to:

- HP;
- Exertion (tactical anti-spam/physical strain);
- a build/source-specific power resource where appropriate (Reserve, Mana, Charge, Favor, Rage, ammunition, etc.);
- Fate;
- Wounds/Injuries;
- Conditions;
- Morale when the encounter/system calls for it.

The old universal Composure pool is not required for every character. Mental/spiritual pressure is handled through saves, conditions, Morale or a specialized track when a build/system genuinely needs one.

Momentum is not a universal shipping requirement; FP-05B permits it only as a prototype candidate if it adds tactical value without duplicating Fate/Morale.

## 10. Fate

Fate is a scarce probability/heroic-agency resource. It may support bounded rerolls, resistance to death/injury, initiative manipulation, Mantle features or exceptional Stunts depending on final tuning.

Fate cannot make an impossible action possible, create nonexistent objects or override scale/permission rules.

## 11. Levels and Power Tiers

Retain Levels 1–20.

Power Tier milestones:

- Levels 1–4 — Emergent
- 5–8 — Established
- 9–12 — Iconic
- 13–16 — Mythic
- 17–20 — Ascendant

Tier raises the normal permission/scale ceiling; it does not level-scale the world or automatically raise every statistic.

## 12. Breakthroughs

Crossing major Tier boundaries should require an in-world Breakthrough in addition to XP: training, experimentation, crisis, invention, transformation, oath, pact, theft, exposure, spiritual realization or another causally coherent path.

There is no mandatory heroic plot gate.

## 13. Powers and techniques

A power family defines:

- core fictional/mechanical permission;
- potency;
- control;
- reach;
- efficiency/resource use;
- known techniques/actions;
- reactions;
- Stunt affordances;
- limits/risks.

Improvement can unlock new actions and combinations rather than only increasing numbers.

## 14. Stunts

Undefined but plausible actions become **Stunts** when they require mechanical adjudication beyond a standard action.

A Stunt proposal identifies grounded entities, intended effect, relevant ability/skill/technique, AP/time/resource cost, difficulty/defense, failure consequences and possible world effects.

Prefer one meaningful resolution sequence over chains of trivial checks.

Stunts are contextual options, not free superior attacks.

## 15. Scale

Power/physical scale affects achievable effect rather than automatically affecting accuracy, intelligence or agency. Weaker characters can threaten stronger entities through vulnerabilities, preparation, environment, relics, Names, combined actions and appropriate equipment.

Scale is not permission for universal level scaling.

## 16. Conditions

Conditions are explicit, versioned mechanical states with defined triggers, effects, duration and removal. Familiar conditions should use familiar language where useful; Mythic-specific supernatural conditions can extend the system.

Avoid hidden modifier soup.

## 17. Equipment

Equipment is physical world state, not an abstract gear-score treadmill.

Weapons/armor can trade damage, AP cost, reach, protection, Exertion burden, movement, stealth and tactical techniques. Items can be owned, stolen, damaged, repaired, inherited, lost or destroyed.

Unique items maintain stable identity and history.

## 18. Advancement

XP can come from combat, objectives, exploration, social achievements, investigation, survival, invention, projects, faction/political change and world-significant achievements.

Killing must not become universally optimal. Repetition/grinding can exist but should have diminishing efficiency.

Build Points/perk choices are retained conceptually as a flexible advancement currency; exact cadence is tuning-gated through simulation/playtesting rather than blindly inherited.

## 19. Morality and reputation

Hero/villain labels are social/world reactions, not alignment rails. Internal moral consequences and audience-specific reputation may coexist.

Morality/reputation changes derive from informed actions and propagated knowledge. Unknown deeds cannot automatically alter public reputation.

## 20. Succession and generation

A successor enters with capabilities created by that person's actual life: age, training, education, powers, injuries, relationships, possessions, claims and experiences.

The successor does not inherit the previous player's Level, private memories or equipment by UI convenience. Any inheritance must occur causally in the world.

## 21. Tactical action economy

FP-05B is authoritative for combat action economy:

- hex grid;
- individual initiative;
- Action Points;
- separate Reaction;
- Exertion;
- LOS/cover/elevation/engagement;
- Morale/injury/retreat/surrender;
- environmental Stunts.

This supersedes the Constitution's older Major Action + Move + Quick Action tactical model.

## 22. Outcome degrees

Margins/degrees may still be used where they improve consequences (e.g., exceptional success or severe failure), but ordinary combat UI should not become overcomplicated. Degree rules must be explicit per subsystem.

## 23. Rule authority

A legal action has a deterministic resolution procedure. The AI may interpret free-form text into a typed proposal, but cannot alter the rule/DC after seeing the roll or directly commit results.

## 24. Replay/versioning

Every canonical random/mechanical resolution must be reproducible from recorded inputs, rules version, tuning version, RNG algorithm/stream/seed and committed state basis.

Balance patches never rewrite historical events.

## 25. Mathematical governance

Completed mechanics must publish:

- deterministic formula/procedure;
- variable definitions/ranges;
- operation/stacking order;
- rounding;
- caps/overflow;
- trigger timing/duration;
- Advantage/Disadvantage/critical interactions;
- impossible-action behavior;
- required replay/event fields;
- rules and tuning versions.

Stable formulas and tuning tables remain separate.

## 26. What remains open for tuning

- exact skill list;
- starting ability generation/point-buy values;
- HP formulas;
- injury/death thresholds;
- exact Fate costs/recharge;
- exact XP curve and Build Point cadence;
- power resource formulas;
- final condition catalogue;
- exact tier/breakthrough rewards.

These are prototype/balance decisions, not reasons to reopen the structural rules architecture.

## 27. Fun gate

A character build must change meaningful tactical/social/exploration decisions rather than merely produce higher percentages. Players should regularly gain new solution space through perks, gear, powers, relationships and world position.

## 28. Legacy reconciliation

For Mythic 2.0 mechanics, the older seven attributes, Edge/Setback universal dice terminology, derived defense set, Major/Move/Quick combat economy, mandatory Composure and universal Momentum requirements are superseded.

Preserved from the World Constitution: no plot armor, deterministic authority, Fate/Power Tier/Mantle/Name/Epithet/Legacy concepts, persistent injury/death, non-level-scaled world adaptation, and rigorous formula/version/replay requirements.

## 29. Authority

Precedence for mechanics:

1. FP-05B tactical combat for encounter action economy/tactics;
2. FP-05 for core character/d20 grammar;
3. FP-02 for player/game/progression loops;
4. World Constitution mechanics only where non-conflicting or explicitly retained.

World Constitution setting/lore remains authoritative unless a later Mythic 2.0 canon decision explicitly supersedes it.
